⚔ Enemy Spawner Mod

Ages of Conflict — Bookmarklet
Installation
1
Make sure your browser's bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome / Edge, ⌘+Shift+B in Safari).
2
Drag the button below onto your bookmarks bar. A new bookmark called "⚔ Enemy Spawner" will appear.
3
Open Ages of Conflict in your browser, then click the bookmark. The mod panel will appear in the top-right corner of the game.
⚔ Enemy Spawner
← drag this to your bookmarks bar
Features
  • Spawn 1–50 enemies at once using the count slider
  • Click-to-Spawn mode — click anywhere on the map to place enemies at that exact world position
  • Random Edge mode — enemies arrive from a random map edge like normal waves
  • Activates waveActive so enemies immediately begin attacking your base
  • Enemies are identical to normal raiders — full AI, HP, attack behaviour
  • Panel styled to match the game's pixel-art HUD
  • Press ESC or click ✕ to close the panel
⚠ Only works on the Ages of Conflict HTML file loaded in a browser tab.
The bookmarklet reads the game's internal enemies, createLegacyFromDefinition, camX, camY, and camZoom variables directly from the page's scope.
